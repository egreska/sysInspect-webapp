## 🛡️ Account Security {#account-security}

### Account Lockout Protection

**How It Works:**
- After **5 failed login attempts**
- Account locks for **15 minutes**
- Protects against brute force attacks

**What You'll See:**
```
Attempt 1: "Invalid password. 4 attempts remaining."
Attempt 2: "Invalid password. 3 attempts remaining."
Attempt 3: "Invalid password. 2 attempts remaining."
Attempt 4: "Invalid password. 1 attempt remaining."
Attempt 5: "Account Locked. Too many failed attempts.
           Try again in 15:00 minutes."
```

**During Lockout:**
- Shows remaining time (e.g., "14:32 remaining")
- Updates every time you check
- Can't login even with correct password
- Use "Forgot Password" to reset immediately

**After Lockout Expires:**
- Automatically unlocked
- Can login normally
- Failed attempts reset to 0

### Security Best Practices

**Password Safety:**
- ✅ Use unique password
- ✅ Don't share with others
- ✅ Change periodically
- ✅ Don't write it down
- ✅ Use password manager

**Account Safety:**
- ✅ Enable biometric login
- ✅ Remember security question answer
- ✅ Logout on shared devices
- ✅ Keep iOS updated
- ✅ Review Settings regularly

## 🔑 Forgot Password {#forgot-password}

### Password Recovery Process

**Complete Step-by-Step Guide:**

#### Step 1: Start Recovery
1. On login screen, tap **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Recovery screen appears

#### Step 2: Enter Email
1. Type your registered email
2. Tap **"Next"**
3. System looks up your account

#### Step 3: Answer Security Question
1. Your security question appears
2. Enter your answer
3. System verifies answer
   - **Correct:** Proceed to reset
   - **Incorrect:** "Security answer incorrect. Please try again."

#### Step 4: Create New Password
1. Enter new password
2. Meet all requirements (see below)
3. Confirm new password
4. System shows password strength

#### Step 5: Complete Reset
1. Tap **"Reset Password"**
2. Password updated immediately
3. Account lockout cleared (if was locked)
4. Automatically logged in
5. Success message: "Password reset successfully!"

### Password Requirements

Your new password must have:
- ✅ **Minimum 8 characters**
- ✅ **1 uppercase letter** (A-Z)
- ✅ **1 lowercase letter** (a-z)
- ✅ **1 number** (0-9)

**Example Valid Passwords:**
- `MyPassword123`
- `SecureApp2024`
- `Inspector2026`

**Example Invalid Passwords:**
- `password` (no uppercase, no number)
- `PASSWORD123` (no lowercase)
- `MyPass` (too short, no number)

### Important Notes

**Security Question Required:**
- Must be set during account creation
- Can't recover without it
- Answers are case-insensitive
- Whitespace is ignored

**What Gets Reset:**
- ✅ Your password
- ✅ Account lockout (if locked)
- ✅ Failed login attempts

**What Stays the Same:**
- ✅ Your email
- ✅ All your data
- ✅ Customer information
- ✅ Inspection history
- ✅ App settings

### Can't Remember Security Answer?

If you can't remember your security answer:
1. Try common variations
2. Think about when you created account
3. Check if you wrote it down
4. **Last resort:** Create new account (data won't transfer)

**💡 Tip:** Write down security answer in secure place!

## ❓ Common Questions {#common-questions}

### Account & Login

**Q: Can I use the app without an account?**
A: No, an account is required for data security and CloudKit sync.

**Q: Can I change my email?**
A: Not currently. Future feature planned.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: You can create multiple accounts with different emails, but can only be logged into one at a time.

**Q: What if I forget both password and security answer?**
A: You'll need to create a new account. Data from old account cannot be recovered.

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: Yes! All features work offline. Data syncs when back online.

### Data & Sync

**Q: Is my data backed up?**
A: Yes, if CloudKit is enabled. All data syncs to your iCloud automatically.

**Q: Can I use the app on multiple devices?**
A: Yes! Sign in with same account on multiple devices. Data syncs via CloudKit.

**Q: What happens if I delete the app?**
A: Your data is safe in CloudKit. Reinstall and login to restore everything.

**Q: How much iCloud storage do I need?**
A: Depends on photo usage. Average: 100-500 MB per 100 inspections.

**Q: Can I disable CloudKit sync?**
A: Yes, but you'll lose cross-device sync and automatic backup.

### Photos

**Q: Where are photos stored?**
A: Primarily in CloudKit (your iCloud). Cached locally for speed.

**Q: Why do photos take longer to load the first time?**
A: First load is from CloudKit (~100-500ms). Subsequent loads are from cache (<1ms).

**Q: Can I delete photos?**
A: Yes, in inspection editor. Deleted photos are removed from CloudKit.

**Q: What photo formats are supported?**
A: JPEG, PNG, HEIF (iPhone's native format), and most common formats.

**Q: Is there a limit on photos per inspection?**
A: No hard limit, but for performance, 20-30 photos per inspection is optimal.

### Performance

**Q: Why does the customer list only show 20 at a time?**
A: Pagination improves performance. More customers load as you scroll.

**Q: How do I make the app faster?**
A: Settings → Release Memory or Clear Image Cache occasionally.

**Q: Why is cache important?**
A: Cache makes photos load 200x faster (from <1ms vs 200ms).

**Q: What's the max cache size?**
A: Memory: 50 MB, Disk: 100 MB. Auto-managed.

### Security

**Q: How secure is my data?**
A: Very secure! Bank-level PBKDF2 encryption, encrypted CloudKit storage, iOS secure storage.

**Q: Can someone hack my account?**
A: Account lockout after 5 failed attempts makes brute force attacks nearly impossible.

**Q: Is biometric login secure?**
A: Yes! Uses iOS secure enclave. Biometric data never leaves your device.

**Q: Are my passwords stored?**
A: No! Only encrypted hashes. Your actual password is never stored.

**Q: What if my device is stolen?**
A: Use Find My iPhone to lock/erase. Change password from another device.

### Reports

**Q: Can I customize reports?**
A: Not currently. Standard professional format. Customization is a planned feature.

**Q: Can I email reports from the app?**
A: Yes! Generate PDF → Share → Email.

**Q: Can I print reports?**
A: Yes! Generate PDF → Share → AirPrint.

**Q: Can I edit reports after generating?**
A: PDF is final. Edit inspection data then regenerate PDF.

### Pricing

**Q: Is the app free?**
A: Check App Store for current pricing and subscriptions.

**Q: Are there in-app purchases?**
A: Check app for current feature availability.

**Q: Do I need to pay for CloudKit?**
A: No, CloudKit is free (uses your iCloud storage which may have limits).

### Support

**Q: How do I get help?**
A: Check this guide first! Settings → Help & Support for more info.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Use App Store → Write a Review or contact support through app.

**Q: When will [feature] be added?**
A: Check for app updates regularly. New features added frequently!
